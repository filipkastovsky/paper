Marshmallow

Soft enough to poke.

The friendliest read of the spec. Four-way pastel palette, plump radii, shadows with colour in them, and display numerals you want to frame on a wall. This is the Duolingo-for-crypto promise delivered at face value — refined, but never serious. The brand says you're safe here, have a snack.

What this direction is for

Users who are nervous about money and need the interface to signal warmth before anything else. Maximum screenshot appeal on a TikTok grid.

Key move

Balance numerals are the hero. Oversized, tabular, slightly italic. They do 70% of the personality work — the rest of the UI can stay quiet.

Loss color

Coral at 28° hue, not red. Softens the sting of a paper-trade that went wrong without faking a win.

A Palette

Cream paper tinted warm. Four equally-weighted pastel hues rotating across feature surfaces so no single colour "owns" a category — avoid the Robinhood-red-means-down trap. Deeper chroma reserved for numerals in celebratory moments.

B Typography

Bricolage Grotesque handles display and UI labels — variable, warm, with subtle humanist cuts in the terminals. Hanken Grotesk for long-form body. Numerals use tabular figures and ss01 so every digit carries the same weight.

Four load-bearing moments for the retention + virality loops: onboarding's $10K reveal, the daily dashboard, a trade confirmation, and a streak-milestone share card. Each one has to survive being cropped to a phone screenshot on mute.
