Fundamentals · Card 02

Why does the price move?

Bitcoin's price is set by supply and demand — the same as anything else you can buy. There's a fixed amount of bitcoin (21 million), so when more people want it than have it, the price goes up.

Key idea Limited supply + fluctuating demand = volatile price. That's the whole story.
