Direction 03 — Riso Print

TWO INKS.
MAX VOLUME.

Printed on cream manila, two risograph inks (fluorescent pink + deep blue) and one ink black. Grain texture, misregistered colour blocks, rubber-stamp moments. The most distinctive direction — and therefore the most polarising. Built to be impossible to mistake for any other fintech on a TikTok grid.

Why the constraints

Limited palette + grain = instant recognisability. Two seconds of a video and you know it's this app.

Typography hierarchy

Boldonse for display — short-burst headlines only. Departure Mono for all numerals — the dot-matrix feel is part of the brand.

Where it struggles

Not for every audience. Reads as cool kid — great if that's the positioning, wrong if you want mass-market Duolingo warmth.

Palette

Four inputs: manila paper, ink black, fluorescent pink, deep blue. Up and down are recoded to blue/pink rather than green/red — a deliberate brand choice that makes gains and losses chromatically equal rather than emotionally unbalanced.

Typography

Boldonse — a recent release with blocky, slightly stencil-like personality — for hero moments and labels. Atkinson Hyperlegible for body copy, chosen specifically for the open terminals that stay readable even with heavy grain overlay. Departure Mono for all numerals — dot-matrix character, immediately legible as "ticker / score / balance".

Screens

Misregistration everywhere — shadows cast in pink, labels stamped, numerals always in Departure Mono with a pink ghost offset. A dashboard here looks like a ticket stub, a trade confirm looks like a receipt.
