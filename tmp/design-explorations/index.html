v0 · design-language explorations

Three ways to look screenshot-worthy.

The spec calls for a beautiful, beginner-friendly paper-trading app — the Duolingo of crypto. This folder contains three distinct design-language drafts that all satisfy the brief but lead the brand toward different emotional territories. Pick one. Merge two. Reject all three. The point is to feel the range before committing.

Direction 02

Editorial

Serif-led, magazine-quiet. Generous whitespace, one sharp terracotta accent, ink-on-paper. The opposite of "finance app" — reads like a design-journal column about money.

Direction 03

Riso Print

Two-ink zine print. Fluorescent pink and deep blue on manila cream, slight grain and misregistration. The most viral-bait direction — instantly recognizable in a TikTok grid.

Boldonse Atkinson Hyperlegible Departure Mono

What each direction is optimising for

Every direction builds the same four screens — onboarding $10K reveal, dashboard, trade confirm+success, and a share card — so you can compare how the same product feels under different brand clothing.

What to notice

  • How balance numbers are typeset — they're the product's hero typography.
  • The loss color. Spec says no alarm-red; each direction answers differently.
  • How the share card reads when cropped to a 9:16 frame on mute.
