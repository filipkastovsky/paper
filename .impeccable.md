# Design Context

Derived from `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-23-neo-fintech-minimalist-exchange-design.md` (approved v0 spec).

## Users

Crypto **beginners** who stumble onto the product from TikTok. They have no finance background, are mobile-first (100%), and are using the app in short 2-minute ritual sessions (daily prediction + one lesson). They are NOT traders, they are NOT sophisticated, and they WILL bounce if the UI looks intimidating or overwhelming.

Secondary: existing users who take **screenshots of wins** and post them back to TikTok / IG / X. The interface is consumed as much through share cards as through the app itself.

## Use Cases / Jobs-to-be-Done

1. **Learn** a crypto concept in 60 seconds (bite-sized card)
2. **Predict** today's market direction (20 seconds, once a day)
3. **Paper-trade** an asset they just learned about (savor the moment, then share)
4. **Check rank** on weekly leaderboard (friends + global)
5. **Share** a milestone (streak, rank, portfolio gain) as a pastel vertical card to TikTok/IG

## Brand Personality

Three words: **friendly · considered · screenshot-worthy**

Reference archetype: "The Duolingo of crypto." Warm and approachable, but not dumbed-down — users trust it with (fake) money. The opposite of Binance/Coinbase chart-heavy aesthetics.

Emotional goals: **confidence without intimidation**, **delight at milestones**, **quiet calm during learning**. Never FOMO, never gambling-adjacent.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Mobile-only**, portrait, PWA. No desktop, no landscape, no tablet adaptation.
- **Light mode only.** Dark mode is an explicit non-goal — contradicts the brand.
- **Pastel** is the PRD default, but the deeper principle is: **soft surfaces, generous whitespace, high-quality typography, and celebratory micro-moments**. Pastel is *one expression* of this; other directions (editorial serif, print-zine) can satisfy the same intent.
- Every gain-moment screen must be **shareable as a 1080×1920 vertical card**.
- Explicit anti-references: candlestick charts, order books, terminal-green ticker aesthetics, Robinhood confetti-explosion, generic Material 3, dark-on-glow AI-slop.

## Design Principles

1. **Celebrate, don't overwhelm.** Every win deserves one big beautiful moment (hero balance reveal, streak milestone, trade success) — not a page full of competing info.
2. **The absence is the differentiation.** No candlesticks, no TA, no order book. Ruthlessly strip information density.
3. **Numbers are typography.** Balances, prices, and streak counts are the hero type — pick numeral treatments with care (tabular, distinctive, readable at a glance).
4. **Screenshot-first.** Every celebratory view must look great cropped to a phone screenshot posted to TikTok.
5. **No intimidation vectors.** Reject candlesticks, red-green flashing, alarm-style warnings, aggressive reds. Loss colors should be coral/salmon, not blood-red.

## Font Policy

The v0 spec currently names Plus Jakarta Sans, Cabinet Grotesk, and Space Grotesk. Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Grotesk are training-default fonts and should be replaced during this design-language exploration. Cabinet Grotesk is acceptable but not the only option.

Valid alternative display fonts to consider: Bricolage Grotesque, Schibsted Grotesk, Gloock, Bagel Fat One, Boldonse, Funnel Display, Climate Crisis, Mona Sans.

Valid body fonts: Hanken Grotesk, Schibsted Grotesk, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Mona Sans.

## Color Policy

Primary brand hues live in pastel lightness range (L ≈ 0.82–0.90) but must reduce chroma when approaching extreme lightness (spec appendix currently over-saturates #E0E7FF at L~0.9). Neutral surfaces must be **tinted toward brand hue** — never pure `#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF`. Loss red should be a coral (hue ≈ 25°), never a fire-engine red.

## Chosen Direction (2026-04-23)

The design language exploration at `tmp/design-explorations/` produced three drafts: Marshmallow, Editorial, Riso Print. **Marshmallow was selected.** It is now canonical:

- Documentation: `docs/design/design-system.md`
- Portable tokens: `docs/design/tokens.css`
- Reference showcase: `tmp/design-explorations/01-marshmallow/index.html`
- Spec Appendix A (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-23-neo-fintech-minimalist-exchange-design.md`) is annotated as superseded by Marshmallow.

Future design work on this project should assume Marshmallow tokens and principles unless explicitly re-litigated. Do not re-run the three-direction exploration unless the product positioning changes.
